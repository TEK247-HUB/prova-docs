## Troubleshooting Guide

### 1. `401 Unauthorized` during SIWE Signature Verification
- **Symptom:** Calling `POST /api/v1/auth/wallet/verify` returns `401 Unauthorized` with `Invalid signature`.
- **Probable Causes:**
  - The authentication nonce expired (nonces expire after 5 minutes).
  - The EIP-4361 message `chainId` did not match `421614` (Arbitrum Sepolia).
  - The address in the SIWE statement does not match the public key recovered from the signature.
- **Diagnostic Steps:**
  1. Inspect the timestamp on `POST /api/v1/auth/wallet/nonce`. If more than 5 minutes have passed, fetch a fresh nonce.
  2. Verify that your wallet client has set `chainId: 421614`.
  3. Ensure the `wallet_address` parameter sent in the verify payload is checksummed or matches the message text exactly.

---

### 2. `409 Conflict: Illegal policy transition`
- **Symptom:** Calling `POST /institutions/:id/policies/:policyId/issue` returns `409 Conflict`.
- **Probable Causes:**
  - The policy is currently in `QUOTED` state and must be accepted before it can be issued.
  - The policy was already cancelled or expired.
- **Diagnostic Steps:**
  1. Query `GET /institutions/:id/policies/:policyId` to inspect the `status` field.
  2. Review the [Policy Lifecycle Guide](/guides/policy-lifecycle). The required sequence is: `DRAFT` → `QUOTED` → `ACCEPTED` → `ISSUED` → `ACTIVE`.
  3. Call `POST /institutions/:id/policies/:policyId/accept` prior to issuing.

---

### 3. Smart Contract Revert: `InvoiceAlreadyRegistered`
- **Symptom:** Calling `ConfidentialEscrow.create()` reverts on-chain with `InvoiceAlreadyRegistered(bytes32)`.
- **Probable Causes:**
  - An escrow with the exact same buyer address, seller address, face amount, and maturity date was already created on `TradeInvoiceResolver`.
- **Diagnostic Steps:**
  1. Inspect the invoice parameters in your ERP or request payload.
  2. The resolver hashes `keccak256(abi.encode(buyer, seller, invoiceAmount, dueDate))` to prevent double-invoicing.
  3. Ensure that your external invoice reference or due date differentiates distinct commercial shipments.

---

### 4. Smart Contract Revert: `StaleOracleScore`
- **Symptom:** Policy quotation or coverage purchase reverts on-chain with `StaleOracleScore`.
- **Probable Causes:**
  - The credit score risk proof submitted by the backend was generated more than `maxScoreStaleness` seconds ago (default 15 minutes).
- **Diagnostic Steps:**
  1. Query `GET /api/v1/credit-score/:buyerAddress` to obtain a fresh `risk_proof` and inspect `expires_at`.
  2. Ensure your backend submits the coverage transaction promptly after receiving the score.

---

### 5. `422 Unprocessable Entity: Validation Failed`
- **Symptom:** Submitting an escrow or policy application returns `422 Unprocessable Entity`.
- **Probable Causes:**
  - `requested_coverage_pct_bps` exceeds `10000` (100%).
  - Invoice `amount` is negative or zero.
  - Date strings do not match the expected `YYYY-MM-DD` ISO format.
- **Resolution:**
  - Check the `invalid_params` array in the response body for the exact offending field name and validation constraint.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Prova an insurance company or custodian?
**No.** Prova is programmable trade-credit infrastructure. Prova contracts are plugins for ReineiraOS. Prova never custodies client deposits, never issues insurance policies as a balance-sheet underwriter, and holds no client funds. Premiums, reserves, and payouts live inside audited decentralized smart contracts.

### Does Prova publish buyer financial information on-chain?
**No.** Sensitive counterparty data (credit limits, credit scores, concentration caps, risk curves) is encrypted using **Fully Homomorphic Encryption (FHE via Fhenix CoFHE)**. Smart contracts perform risk math on encrypted ciphertexts (`euint64`, `euint32`). Plaintext values are never published to public blockchain state.

### Which currencies are supported?
The testnet implementation uses **USDC** and **cUSDC** (confidential wrapped USDC) on Arbitrum Sepolia. The `ConfidentialEscrow` engine is token-agnostic and designed to support any compliant `IFHERC20` confidential stablecoin.

### How do I get testnet tokens for Arbitrum Sepolia?
1. Obtain Arbitrum Sepolia testnet ETH from public faucets (e.g., QuickNode or Alchemy Sepolia faucets).
2. For testnet USDC, interact with the testnet token contract at `0x75faf114eafb1BDbe2F0316DF893fd58CE46AA4d` or use Prova's authenticated seed endpoint `POST /api/v1/demo/seed` to configure sandbox testing balances.
