## What is Prova?

**Prova** is developer infrastructure for trade-credit insurance. Financial institutions, trade credit insurers, commercial banks, export credit agencies, factoring firms, and risk syndicates plug into Prova to issue, manage, and settle invoice protection on-chain.

<Note type="info" title="Infrastructure, not an insurer">
  Prova is **non-custodial, non-risk-bearing, unregulated, and not an insurance company, underwriter, or capital custodian**. Prova authors smart contract plugins that emit cryptographically verified underwriting decisions. Pooled capital, premiums, and settlement payouts reside exclusively inside decentralized protocol contracts on Arbitrum Sepolia; Prova never custodies or moves client funds.
</Note>

## The problem Prova solves

Traditional trade-credit insurance (TCI) is slow, opaque, and inaccessible to high-growth corridors:
- **Lengthy paper underwriting:** Traditional policy issuance takes weeks of financial statement audits, leaving modern supply chains unprotected.
- **Fragmented settlement:** Claims for buyer protracted default take 90 to 180 days to settle through manual claims adjustment and wire transfers.
- **Privacy vs. on-chain transparency:** Institutions cannot publish debtor identities, credit limits, or proprietary risk pricing to public blockchains.
- **Concentration risk:** Uncoordinated lending across multiple factoring desks leads to undisclosed debtor overexposure and sudden defaults.

Prova resolves these challenges by bridging **off-chain risk scoring** with **on-chain confidential execution**:
1. **Confidential Underwriting:** Sensitive credit limits, premium curves, and buyer risk scores are encrypted with Fully Homomorphic Encryption (FHE via Fhenix CoFHE). Math is computed on encrypted values without decrypting them on-chain.
2. **Deterministic Settlement:** Invoice escrows release automatically upon maturity or trigger claim windows for protracted default.
3. **Tamper-Proof Solvency Guardrails:** Aggregate debtor exposure is bounded by smart contracts across all pools simultaneously, preventing credit limit evasion through pool splitting.

## Core architecture & system boundaries

Prova enforces a strict architectural boundary: **on-chain only what requires blockchain trust** (authority to release pooled capital, immutable public settlement, or tamper-proof solvency caps). Everything the backend can be trusted to coordinate remains off-chain.

## Intended users & integration scenarios

- **Trade Credit Insurers:** Digitize existing underwriting guidelines into programmable premium curves and add-on matrices; automate issuance and claim validation.
- **B2B Marketplaces & Platforms:** Protect supplier receivables at checkout by embedding programmatic invoice escrows with attached credit protection.
- **Factoring Companies & Lenders:** Hedge non-payment risk on purchased invoices by binding coverage policies before advancing funds.
- **Liquidity Providers & Underwriting Syndicates:** Stake capital into segregated risk pools and earn real-yield premiums on insured trade flows.
